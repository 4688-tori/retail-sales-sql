# Business Insights – Retail Sales & Customer Intelligence

## 1. Mobile Phones generate the highest revenue

**Finding:** Mobile Phones generated the highest revenue at KSh 6,942,060.65, followed by Computing at KSh 6,596,221.70. Accessories generated the lowest revenue at KSh 1,077,496.10.

**Why it matters:** Mobile Phones and Computing are the strongest revenue-generating categories. Accessories contribute less revenue, but this does not necessarily mean they are unprofitable.

**Recommendation:** Maintain sufficient stock of Mobile Phones and Computing products. Consider bundling accessories with high-demand products to increase accessory sales.

## 2. Nairobi contributes the largest share of revenue

**Finding:** Nairobi generated KSh 9,581,980.35 in completed-order revenue, representing approximately 39.67% of total revenue. Machakos recorded the lowest revenue at KSh 745,099.90.

**Why it matters:** The business depends considerably on Nairobi for its sales performance. Lower-revenue locations may represent opportunities for growth.

**Recommendation:** Maintain the business's strong presence in Nairobi while investigating opportunities to increase sales in Machakos and other lower-revenue locations.

## 3. VIP customers have the highest average spending

**Finding:** Average completed-order spending per purchasing customer was:
- VIP: KSh 253,251.88
- Corporate: KSh 197,958.71
- Retail: KSh 163,575.21

**Why it matters:** VIP customers generate the highest average spending, making them an important customer segment for revenue generation.

**Recommendation:** Introduce loyalty rewards and personalised offers for VIP customers. Encourage repeat purchases among Retail customers through promotions and product recommendations.

## 4. Accessories have the highest profit margin

**Finding:** Accessories generated KSh 1,077,496.10 in revenue and KSh 456,746.10 in profit, giving them a profit margin of approximately 42.39%. Mobile Phones generated the highest absolute profit at KSh 728,760.65.

**Why it matters:** Although Accessories generate the lowest revenue, they have the highest profit margin among the five categories. Revenue alone does not indicate profitability.

**Recommendation:** Explore opportunities to increase accessory sales while maintaining their margins. Continue monitoring the profitability of high-revenue categories such as Mobile Phones.

## 5. A significant number of customers are inactive

**Finding:** 47 customers had not made a completed purchase for more than 90 days as of 30 September 2026. Another 18 customers had no completed purchases in the dataset.

**Why it matters:** Inactive customers represent potential lost sales opportunities. Customers without completed purchases may also indicate difficulties in converting registrations into sales.

**Recommendation:** Introduce customer re-engagement campaigns, special offers and follow-up communication. Investigate why registered customers have not completed purchases.

## 6. Monthly revenue increased significantly in February 2026

**Finding:** Completed-order revenue increased from KSh 322,432.95 in January 2026 to KSh 762,338.85 in February 2026, representing month-on-month growth of approximately 136.43%.

**Why it matters:** Significant changes in monthly revenue can indicate changes in customer demand, promotions or sales performance.

**Recommendation:** Investigate the factors that contributed to the February increase and identify successful activities that could be repeated in subsequent months.

---

## Conclusion

The analysis shows that Mobile Phones and Computing are major revenue contributors, while Accessories provide the highest profit margin. Nairobi is the leading revenue-generating city, and VIP customers have the highest average spending. The business should focus on customer retention, regional growth opportunities and maintaining product profitability to improve overall performance.
